Home Clean Home — Privacy Policy

Who is responsible

The data controller is Tutatechno, a sole trader registered in Hungary. Correspondence, including any request under data-protection law: tutatechno@gmail.com. Full registered details are held by Google Play and are supplied on request to any user or supervisory authority entitled to them.

Scope

This policy covers the Home Clean Home Android application. It forms part of the Terms of Use.

What the app stores, and where

The following is created and held on your device, in the app's isolated storage (localStorage), which no other application can read:

DataPurpose
Nickname (optional)Greeting on the home screen
CountryDetermining which shops are shown
Home details: rooms, floors, sketch, windows, marked spotsSizing the cleaning plan
Close-up photos (optional)Recording a surface or a stain
Task history, calendar, points, streakThe plan and your progress
Kit and shopping listConsumption estimates and suggestions
Settings: language, theme, cleanliness level, daily load, reminder timeRunning the app

The reminder time and text are kept in the app's own preferences file. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

Permissions

PermissionPurpose
INTERNETRequired by the app's structure: the interface runs in an embedded browser view that loads files stored on the device.
POST_NOTIFICATIONSThe daily reminder, if enabled. Off by default.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDSo a reminder survives a device restart.

The app requests no camera or storage permission. When you add a photo, Android's own picker or camera opens and the app receives only the image you select.

Photos

Photos are scaled to 900 pixels and compressed on your device, then stored in the app's own storage. They are not analysed and not uploaded. You can delete them by deleting the marked spot, or by deleting all data.

Shops and affiliate links

The app suggests cleaning products and offers links to online shops. Some are affiliate links: if you buy through them the shop may pay us a commission, at no additional cost to you.

Tapping such a link leaves the app and opens the shop in your phone's browser. From that point the shop and its affiliate network may set their own cookies or identifiers in order to attribute the sale. That processing is carried out by them, under their own privacy policies. We receive aggregated commission reports only, which do not identify you, your address or the contents of your basket.

Product suggestions can be turned off under More → Product tips.

Export and deletion

Under More → My data you can export everything to a JSON file in your Downloads folder, import a previous export, or delete everything with one tap.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection over personal data a controller holds about you. Everything the app stores is held on your device and under your direct control; you can delete it inside the app, through Android's Settings → Apps → Storage → Clear storage, or by uninstalling.

If you contact us by e-mail, we process your e-mail address and the content of your message in order to answer you, on the basis of legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)), and retain it for at most 12 months after replying.

You may lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority. In Hungary this is the National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH, 1055 Budapest, Falk Miksa utca 9-11.).

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes
