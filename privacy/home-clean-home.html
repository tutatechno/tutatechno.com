Home Clean Home — Privacy Policy

Home Clean Home keeps everything on your phone. There is no sign-up, no account, and the app sends nothing to us. Your rooms, your floor sketch, your photos and your progress stay on the device.

Because the app never transmits anything to us, most data-protection questions simply do not arise. This page still sets out exactly what happens.

What the app handles

All of the following is created and kept solely on your device, in the app's own private storage. We never receive, see or store any of it.

Technically all of this is held in a single entry in the app's isolated storage (localStorage), which no other app can read. The reminder time and text are kept in the app's own preferences file.

What the app does not do

  • No registration and no user account.
  • No data sent to any server — ours or anyone else's.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no ad network.
  • No third-party tracking libraries. The app uses only Google's official AndroidX components.
  • No access to your location, contacts, calendar, calls or messages.
  • No use of the Advertising ID.

The app requests no camera or storage permission. When you add a close-up photo, Android's own picker or camera opens, and the app receives only the single image you choose.

Photos

Photos are processed on your phone: the app scales them to 900 pixels, compresses them and stores them in its own storage. Photos never leave the device, are not analysed, and are never uploaded. You can delete them at any time by deleting the marked spot, or by deleting all data.

Shops and affiliate links

The app suggests cleaning products and tools, and offers links to online shops. These are affiliate links: if you buy through them, the shop may pay us a commission. The price you pay is exactly the same.

When you tap such a link you leave the app and your phone's browser opens the shop. From that point the shop and its affiliate network may set their own cookies or identifiers in order to attribute the sale. That processing is theirs, under their own privacy policies — not ours. We receive only aggregated commission reports: we never learn your name, your address or what was in your basket.

If you would rather not see product suggestions at all, turn them off under More → Product tips.

Exporting and deleting your data

Under More → My data you can at any time:

  • Export everything to a JSON file, saved to your phone's Downloads folder and shared with nobody;
  • Import a previous export;
  • Delete everything with one tap.

Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data from your phone. Since we hold nothing about you, there is nothing to request from us — but if you have a question, write to tutatechno@gmail.com.

Legal basis and your rights

The app carries out no processing that brings personal data to us, so our activity as a controller is effectively empty. If you do contact us by e-mail, we process your e-mail address and the content of your message in order to answer you, on the basis of legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)), and keep it for at most 12 months after replying.

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. You may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. In Hungary this is the National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH, 1055 Budapest, Falk Miksa utca 9-11.).

Retention

Data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. Nothing is stored by us.

Children

The app is not designed for children and is not directed at users under 13. We knowingly collect no data about children — as we collect none about anyone.

Security

Because the data never leaves your device, it is protected by Android's built-in app sandbox: no other app can read this app's storage. We recommend keeping a screen lock on your phone, since your data is exactly as secure as the device itself.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects data handling, we will update this policy and change the date at the top. Material changes will also be announced inside the app. If a future version were ever to send any data off the device, it would only do so with your prior, explicit consent, which you could withdraw at any time.
