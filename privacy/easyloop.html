EasyLoop — Privacy Policy

This page is the same text the app shows under Settings → About & legal. See also the Terms of Use.

EasyLoop has no account, and your recordings never leave your phone unless you export or share them yourself. The only thing the app uses the internet for is the advertising banners.

Advertising

The free version shows banner ads supplied by Google AdMob. Ads are a third party inside the app, so be clear about what that means: to fill a banner, the Google Mobile Ads SDK contacts Google's servers and sends what it needs for that — your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, device model, screen size, language, and which app requested the ad. It may also use that data to measure whether ads were seen and to limit how often the same one is shown. That processing is Google's, under Google's own privacy policy, not ours: policies.google.com/privacy. We never receive it.

A one-time purchase to take the adverts away is planned but is not available yet. When it ships, buying it will stop all of this: no advert requested, the advertising SDK not asked to fill anything. This page will be updated before that happens.

Before an ad is requested you are asked for consent, through Google's own consent form. Say no to personalised ads and AdMob serves contextual ones instead, which use less. You can change that answer later: Settings, then Ad privacy options. You can also reset or delete the advertising ID itself in Android's own settings, under Privacy, Ads.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no usage statistics and no crash reporting. We do not run a server, and there is no record of you anywhere in our hands to look at, sell or lose. The only network traffic the app makes is the ad request described above, and it goes to Google, not to us.

What is stored, and where

  • Your recordings, kept untouched so any edit can be undone.
  • Your projects: tempo, loop lengths, effects, volumes, positions, colours.
  • Your sound library.
  • Your settings, including the audio latency measured for your phone, and your consent choice.

All of it lives in the app's private storage, which no other app can read, and none of it is uploaded. Uninstalling deletes all of it.

Exports are the deliberate exception: a finished WAV, MP3 or M4A is written to your phone's Music folder, in "EasyLoop Exports", so your music player and file manager can find it and so it survives uninstalling the app. From that moment it is an ordinary file of yours, and once you share one it follows the rules of wherever you sent it. You can rename or delete exports from inside the app, on the Export screen.

The microphone

Open only while a recording is running, and only to capture the take you asked for. Not used in the background, not used to listen while you are not recording, and never sent anywhere — recorded audio is not part of any ad request. Swiping the app out of recents closes it and releases the microphone at once.

Permissions, and why each one is there

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): to record the sounds you play.
  • Internet and network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): to load the adverts. Nothing else uses them.
  • Storage (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE), on Android 9 and older only: to write your exports into the Music folder. Newer Android versions need no permission for this.
  • Notifications and foreground service (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE): to keep playing with the screen off, with a notification so it is never running invisibly.

Children

A general-audience music app, not directed at children under 13, and it is not in the Play Families programme. Because of the advertising it is not suitable for children under 13.

Your rights

Access, correction, erasure and the rest apply to data somebody holds about you. We hold none. For the data the ad SDK sends to Google, Google is the controller and its privacy policy describes how to exercise those rights; withdrawing consent in Ad privacy options is the fastest lever you have inside the app. Everything on your device is yours to remove: delete a project, use Restore default settings, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall.

Planned features

Online collaboration would have to upload the sounds in a shared project. Nothing of the sort exists today, and this text will be updated before it ships.
