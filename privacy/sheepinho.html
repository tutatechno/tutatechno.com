Sheepinho — Privacy Policy

Who is responsible

The data controller is Tutatechno, a sole trader registered in Hungary. Correspondence, including any request under data-protection law: tutatechno@gmail.com. Full registered details are held by Google Play and are supplied on request to any user or supervisory authority entitled to them.

Scope

This policy covers the Sheepinho Android game (com.tutatechno.sheepinho). It forms part of the Terms of Use.

What the game stores, and where

The game collects no personal data. The following is held in the game's private storage on your device, which no other application can read:

  • Progress. Which levels you have unlocked and completed, and whether you finished the tutorial.
  • Settings. Volume levels, graphics quality, effects, frame-rate limit and game speed.

None of this leaves your device. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage, removes everything it stored.

No advertising, analytics or purchases

The game contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking, no in-app purchases and no user accounts. The Unity engine's analytics, crash-reporting and performance-reporting services are switched off. The game does not send gameplay or device data to Tutatechno or to anyone else.

Permissions

The game requests no runtime permissions — no location, microphone, camera, contacts or storage access. The links under Settings → Legal open this website in your web browser; from that point the browser handles the connection under its own rules.

Third-party components

The game is built with the Unity engine (Unity Technologies) and TextMesh Pro. Both run on your device. The game's graphics, 3D models, music and sounds are Tutatechno's own or used under licence.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection over personal data a controller holds about you. Everything the game stores is held on your device and under your direct control; you can delete it through Android's Settings → Apps → Storage → Clear storage, or by uninstalling.

If you contact us by e-mail, we process your e-mail address and the content of your message in order to answer you, on the basis of legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)), and retain it for at most 12 months after replying.

You may lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority. In Hungary this is the National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH, 1055 Budapest, Falk Miksa utca 9-11.).

Children

The game is intended for a general audience. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children, and shows no advertising.

Changes

The date at the top of this page identifies the current version. If the game changes in a way that affects data handling, this page is updated and material changes are noted in the game's release notes.
