TellWay — Privacy Policy

Who is responsible

The data controller is Tutatechno, a sole trader registered in Hungary. Correspondence, including any request under data-protection law: tutatechno@gmail.com. Full registered details are held by Google Play and are supplied on request to any user or supervisory authority entitled to them.

Scope

This policy covers the TellWay Android application. It forms part of the Terms of Use.

Location

The app uses your precise location to identify places near you. Your coordinates are sent to the search APIs of open databases — Wikipedia, Wikivoyage, Wikidata and OpenStreetMap — which return what is nearby. The coordinates are not retained after the response arrives, on the device or elsewhere; no route log or location history is created.

Precise location is required because the app runs continuously while you travel: it refreshes your position several times a minute so that a story begins at the right moment, and it supports a minimum search radius so that the same places are not repeated on a route driven daily. Coarse location, with an error of one to three kilometres, cannot support either.

While a journey is running the app uses a foreground service, shown as a notification. It runs only during a journey you started and stops when you pause it or remove the app from recents. The app does not request background location permission.

If you start navigation, the coordinates of the destination are passed to the map application on your device. Your own position is not.

Your GPS coordinates are never passed to the advertising SDK. See Advertising below for what the advertising SDK does process.

What the app stores on your device

  • Settings — language, voice, persona, speech rate, topics, audience, appearance, search radius, story length.
  • Saved places — the name and coordinates of places you starred. These are the coordinates of places, not of you.
  • Optional API keys you supply for the optional data sources.
  • Your advertising-consent choice, stored by the Google User Messaging Platform so that you are not asked again on every launch.

The app itself does not store your location, movement history, route, name or account data. Settings → Reset everything removes everything the app has stored, as does uninstalling. The advertising SDK keeps its own data, described below; the way to withdraw consent is Settings → Ad privacy settings inside the app.

Advertising

TellWay is free and is funded by advertising. Ads are supplied by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland), which acts as an independent controller for the data it processes for advertising. Google's own privacy policy applies to that processing: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

What the advertising SDK processes

To request and display an ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and transmit:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, or a non-resettable app identifier where that is unavailable);
  • your IP address, from which an approximate, city-level location is derived;
  • device and app information — model, operating-system version, language, screen size, the app's identifier and version;
  • your interaction with the ads — whether an ad was shown, viewed or tapped.

This happens regardless of your consent choice, because without it no ad can be delivered at all. What your choice decides is whether that data may also be used to build an interest profile and to personalise the ads you see.

Consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent dialog from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before the first ad is requested. It lists Google and its advertising partners and asks for your choice.

  • If you consent, the advertising identifier and the data above may be used by Google and the partners you accepted to personalise ads and measure them, and the approximate location derived from your IP address may be used for advertising purposes. The legal basis is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)).
  • If you refuse, you still see ads, but they are not personalised. Non-personalised ads still use the IP address and basic device information for serving, frequency capping, aggregated reporting and fraud prevention.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time, without giving a reason, in Settings → Ad privacy settings inside the app, which reopens the same dialog. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that already took place. You can also reset or delete the advertising identifier itself in Android's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Speech

Stories are spoken by your device's own text-to-speech engine. If your system is set to a network voice, that service may transmit the text to its own servers in order to synthesise it. That is a device setting rather than an app setting, and it can be changed in Android system settings.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection over personal data a controller holds about you. Everything this app itself stores is held on your device and under your direct control; you can delete it inside the app, through Android's Settings → Apps → Storage → Clear storage, or by uninstalling. For the data Google processes for advertising, exercise your rights with Google through its privacy policy; in the app, withdrawing consent in Settings → Ad privacy settings stops personalised advertising.

If you contact us by e-mail, we process your e-mail address and the content of your message in order to answer you, on the basis of legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)), and retain it for at most 12 months after replying.

You may lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority. In Hungary this is the National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH, 1055 Budapest, Falk Miksa utca 9-11.).
