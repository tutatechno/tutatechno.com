TellWay — Privacy Policy

TellWay has no account, no analytics and no server of ours. Your settings stay on your phone. Your location does leave the device — it is sent to open databases so they can tell us what is near you — but it is never stored, anywhere.

Location

The app uses your precise location to find places near you. Your coordinates are sent to the search APIs of open databases — Wikipedia, Wikivoyage, Wikidata, OpenStreetMap — so they can return what is nearby.

We do not store these coordinates anywhere, on the phone or elsewhere; they are gone once the response arrives. We keep no location history and cannot reconstruct where you have been.

Precise location is required because the app runs continuously while you travel: it refreshes your position several times a minute so a story starts at the right moment, and it lets you set a minimum search radius so the same places are not repeated on a route you drive every day. Coarse location, with an error of one to three kilometres, would make both of those meaningless.

While a journey is running, the app uses a foreground service — the notification in your status bar. That is what lets it keep reading to you with the screen off. It runs only during a journey you started, and stops when you pause it or swipe the app away. The app does not request background location permission.

If you tap the navigate button, the coordinates of the destination are handed to the map application on your phone. Your own location is not.

What is stored on your phone

All of this lives in the app's private storage. Nothing is synced anywhere.

  • Settings — language, voice, persona, speech rate, topics, audience, appearance, search radius, story length.
  • Starred places — the name and coordinates of places you saved. These are the coordinates of places, a castle or a church, not of you.
  • Your own optional API keys, if you enable any of the optional data sources.

Not stored: your location, your movement history, your route, any device identifier, any advertising identifier, e-mail, name or account data.

Settings → Reset everything removes all of it in one tap, as does uninstalling the app.

Third parties

The app queries the following public services. Each has its own privacy policy, and — as with any internet request — your IP address reaches them along with the request.

ServiceWhat is sentWhen
Wikimedia Foundation (Wikipedia, Wikivoyage, Wikidata)Coordinates, search terms, languageAlways
OpenStreetMap and Overpass API operatorsCoordinates, radius, map tile coordinatesAlways
Europeana, GeoNames, OpenTripMapCoordinates, radiusOnly if you enable them

Every request is made over HTTPS. The app sends no device identifier, no advertising ID, no cookie, no account data and no unique user identifier with any of them.

Speech

Stories are spoken by your phone's own text-to-speech engine. If your system is set to a network voice, that service may send the text to its own servers in order to synthesise it. That is your phone's setting rather than ours, and it can be changed in your Android system settings.

Sources and licences

The content you hear comes from open databases and is credited in the app on each place's detail screen: Wikipedia and Wikivoyage under CC BY-SA, Wikidata under CC0, OpenStreetMap under ODbL. The map carries the OpenStreetMap contributors attribution.

Children

The content can be set to be child-friendly, but the app is intended for adult users — typically the driver travelling with children. We do not collect data about children, as we collect none about anyone.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection over personal data a controller holds about you. We hold none: there is no server, no database and no account associated with this app, so there is no record of you to hand over, correct or delete. Everything the app keeps is on your device and under your direct control.

If you contact us by e-mail, we process your e-mail address and the content of your message solely in order to answer you, on the basis of legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)), and keep it for at most 12 months after replying.

You may lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority. In Hungary this is the National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH, 1055 Budapest, Falk Miksa utca 9-11.).

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects data handling, this page is updated and the date at the top changes with it. Material changes are also announced inside the app.
